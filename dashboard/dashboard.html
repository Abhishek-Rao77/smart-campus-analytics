How the Student Success Score works

The score combines six indicators measured on every student, each scaled 0–100 and blended with fixed weights. It answers one question: how likely is this student to succeed academically and in placements without intervention?

The six indicators

IndicatorWeightWhat it measures
Academic35%CGPA (70%) + average of semester marks (30%), minus 10 points per backlog
Attendance20%Overall attendance percentage
LMS activity15%Assignment completion (50%) + quiz scores (30%) + login regularity (20%)
Placement readiness15%Aptitude score (40%) + coding score (40%) + mock interviews (20%)
Skills10%Average of technical, soft-skill and communication assessments
Engagement5%Events, clubs, hackathons and certifications, capped at a full score

Risk rules

  • Academic risk — score below 50, or 2+ backlogs, or attendance below 60%.
  • Placement risk — placement-readiness below 45 and the student is in year 3 or 4.

Segments

  • At-risk — flagged by the academic-risk rule; needs immediate intervention.
  • High academics, low placement — CGPA ≥ 8.5 but placement-readiness below 45; strong students who may miss placements.
  • High engagement, weak academics — highly active on campus (engagement ≥ 60) but CGPA below 6.5.
  • On track — everyone else.

Explainability

On each student's detail view, every indicator is shown as its weighted contribution (weight × indicator value), so faculty can see exactly which factors pull a score up or down — and the platform suggests interventions matched to the weakest indicators.
